What We Do

GroundLink is a communications consultancy. We help organisations keep their links working and secure: radio, satellite, networks and the cyber security around them. We advise, improve what you have or build what is missing, install it and train your people to use it. Take one step, or all four.

Capabilities

01

Advise

An independent review of how your organisation communicates, with written findings and a prioritised list of what to fix first.

02

Improve or build

We tighten the communication plan and procedures you already have, or design new ones with a main link and fallbacks.

03

Install

Radio, satellite and network equipment set up, configured securely and tested at your site, with handover documents your team can follow.

04

Train

Hands-on sessions that teach your people to use the equipment, follow the procedures and handle accounts and devices safely.

Cyber security is part of every step: we also strengthen the accounts, devices and networks your communications depend on.

How We Work

GroundLink is a small firm with a network of independent specialists in radio, networks and cyber security. You get one contract and one point of contact. Each proposal states the scope, the timing and who does the work — a single consultant or a small team, remote or at your site.
